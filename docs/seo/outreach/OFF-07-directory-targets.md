The single best opportunity found. Globally recognized, high-authority
platform specifically for cosmetic/aesthetic procedures, with a hair
transplant category. Free to claim, vets the doctor's actual medical
license, and reviews can't be paid to disappear — which is exactly what
makes a link from it worth having. It's also a genuine patient lead-gen
channel, not just a backlink.

- Claim page: https://www.realself.com/dr/claimprofile

### Marham (marham.pk)
Pakistan's largest doctor directory — 16,000+ doctors, every profile
verified against PMDC registration. Already has a live "Hair Transplant
Surgeons in Islamabad" category page, so the topical + geographic fit is
exact.

- Category page (context): https://www.marham.pk/doctors/islamabad/hair-transplant-surgeon
