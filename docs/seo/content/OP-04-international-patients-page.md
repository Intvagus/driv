### Section: Visa & Entry Requirements

> Foreign nationals traveling to Pakistan for treatment need a **medical visa**. Pakistan offers:
> - **Short-term medical visa**: single entry, valid up to 3 months, typically processed within 48 hours through a Pakistani mission abroad.
> - **Long-term medical visa**: multiple entry, valid up to 1 year, approved by the Ministry of Interior (around 30 days).
>
> Required documents typically include: a valid passport, proof of accommodation in Pakistan, a medical report, and a consultation/consent letter from the treating hospital. [CONFIRM: can the clinic issue this letter / is it on Pakistan's enlisted hospitals list — see note above]
>
> *This is general visa guidance, not legal advice — requirements can change and vary by nationality. Confirm current requirements with your nearest Pakistani embassy or consulate before booking travel.*
