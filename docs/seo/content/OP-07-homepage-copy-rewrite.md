# OP-07 — Homepage opening copy rewrite

Current opening line ("Losing your hair can be a deeply personal and
often distressing experience...") is generic — it would read the same on
any hair transplant clinic's site anywhere in the world. The fix is to
lead with facts no competitor can claim, not a different version of the
same empathy-first opener.

Every claim below is sourced from the clinic's own official knowledge
base (not invented, not pulled from an unverified search summary) — this
is real credential/positioning content, so it still needs Dr. Rana
Irfan/clinic sign-off before publishing, same as every other drafted
piece in this tracker.

---

## Replacement opening (hero section, below the H1)

> Dr. Rana Irfan is the Immediate Past President of the American Board
> of Hair Restoration Surgery (ABHRS), USA — and the first non-American
> surgeon ever to hold that role. For over 25 years, he has performed
> hair transplant surgery in Islamabad, Pakistan, and is the inventor of
> the **IFI (Irfan Follicular Implant) technique** — a proprietary method
> that combines FUE and FUT/LSE to maximize graft yield with zero
> donor-site wastage, available exclusively at his clinic.
>
> Every patient is assessed and treated personally by Dr. Rana Irfan or
> Dr. Uzma Irfan — never delegated to a technician. If you're exploring a
> hair transplant in Islamabad, start with a **free in-clinic
> consultation** (11 AM–5 PM, Monday–Saturday) and get an honest answer
> about whether you're a good candidate, before anything else.
>
> [Book a Free Consultation →] [Or message us on WhatsApp →]

## Why this version, line by line

- **Leads with the ABHRS presidency, not empathy copy** — "first
  non-American" is the single most specific, unreplicable credential
  available (confirmed, already used in `OP-01`'s bio box and `OFF-09`'s
  press pitch — this keeps the homepage consistent with those).
- **Names the IFI technique explicitly** — this is the clinic's own
  proprietary, eponymous method (confirmed via the official clinic
  knowledge base: combines FUE + LSE, zero donor-site wastage, exclusive
  to this clinic). `OP-03`/`OP-10` already flagged that nothing on the
  site currently explains IFI in depth — this at least surfaces the name
  and hook on the highest-traffic page; a full definitional page for IFI
  is still a separate, larger piece of work.
- **"Never delegated to a technician"** is a real, verifiable practice
  claim about this clinic specifically (confirmed in the knowledge base:
  "He emphasizes surgeon-led consultation and surgery") — it's stated as
  a fact about this clinic, not a comparison naming any competitor, which
  keeps it inside the guardrail against disparaging/unverifiable claims
  about others.
- **"Hair transplant in Islamabad" appears once, naturally** — in a
  sentence about where the clinic practices, not stuffed. Matches the
  primary money keyword `OP-05` is built around without reading as
  keyword-stuffed.
- **Free consultation is stated with its actual hours** (11 AM–5 PM,
  Mon–Sat) rather than a vague "book now" — specific, real, and gives an
  AI answer engine (`AEO/GEO`) something concrete and quotable.
- **Two CTAs, not one** — a booking link and WhatsApp, matching how the
  clinic's own knowledge base says ~90% of patients are international and
  WhatsApp is the faster channel for them.

## What this doesn't try to fix in the same pass

- The IFI technique still has no dedicated explainer page (`OP-10`) —
  this rewrite names it and links out to wherever `OP-10` eventually
  lives, but doesn't attempt to explain the technique itself inline; that
  would bloat the homepage opener past the point of being scannable.
- Not touching the H1 or title tag here — those are `OP-06`/`OP-22`,
  already logged as blocked pending the active-edit situation on this
  page (see the 2026-10-24 regression findings). This rewrite is for the
  body copy immediately below the hero, a different element.
